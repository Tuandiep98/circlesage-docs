CircleSage ("CircleSage", "we", "us") is a relationship‑companion app that offers AI‑assisted coaching, reminders, and tools to help you nurture the relationships around you. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

If you have any questions, contact us at tuandiep98usa@gmail.com.

1. Accounts work without a login

CircleSage does not ask you to create a username or password. When you first open the app, we create an anonymous account with our backend provider (Supabase). This account is tied to the app installation, not to your identity.

To help you recover your data after reinstalling or switching devices, the app may send a one‑way SHA‑256 hash of basic device characteristics (for example, the device model and vendor identifier) to our backend. We do not receive the raw identifiers themselves. On iOS, the encryption key used for your encrypted backups may be stored in the iCloud Keychain, so it can sync across your own Apple devices.

2. Information you provide

  • Your profile: display name, birthday, an optional work/role label, and a short description you choose to add.
  • People you add ("connections"): information you enter about the people in your life — such as their name, relationship, birthday, interests, milestones, and optional notes. This can include sensitive details like allergens, foods to avoid, and personal quirks. You should only enter information about another person if you have their permission to do so.
  • Chat messages: anything you type in conversations with the AI assistant.
  • Period tracking (optional): if you turn on period tracking for a connection, we store the dates you confirm or correct for that person's cycle, the start and end dates the app predicts, and whether a prediction turned out accurate — used to improve future predictions and time reminders.
  • AI response reports: when you choose to report one AI response, we receive the selected reason, your optional note, that AI response, your immediately preceding message, limited app context (such as platform, locale, and provider hint), and the screen capture shown in the report sheet unless you remove it. We do not include the rest of the conversation.

3. Information collected automatically

  • Location (precise, including in the background): with your permission, the app monitors your device location and physical‑activity signals to learn your "routine places" (for example, where you usually spend time). This helps the app offer relevant, time‑aware suggestions. Raw location observations are processed and stored on your device; place names are resolved on‑device. A coarse routine label is synced to our backend. If you turn on the separate, off‑by‑default place‑name permission for a linked partner, the on‑device name of a favourite place may also be synced; Home and Work are never shared as a name. If both you and a linked partner explicitly enable proximity suggestions, a precise coordinate representing the center of your current routine place may also be synced temporarily. See Section 8 for details.
  • Device and notification data: a randomly generated device ID, push notification token, platform, timezone, and language — used to deliver notifications and sync your settings.
  • Subscription data: if you purchase a subscription, our payments partner (RevenueCat) processes your purchase using an app‑specific account identifier.

4. How we use your data

We use your data to: provide AI coaching and suggestions; deliver reminders and notifications; personalize responses based on your profile and connections; enforce usage quotas; and maintain and improve the app.

5. Where your data is stored

  • On your device: most of your data — your profile, connections, conversations, AI "memory", and routine places — lives in a local database on your device.
  • Encrypted cloud backup: your conversations, messages, and routine places are encrypted on your device (AES‑256‑GCM) before being uploaded to our backend. We store only the ciphertext and cannot read this content.
  • Connection data for partner linking: when you link with another CircleSage user, the relevant connection details are stored on our backend in plain text so both linked people can see the shared information. Only link with people you trust with that information.
  • Period tracking data: the dates, predictions, and accuracy feedback described in Section 2 are stored on our backend in readable (non‑encrypted) form, so we can compute predictions and schedule reminder notifications on our servers. This data is never included in what a linked partner can see, even if you link with them.
  • Moderation reports: AI response reports are stored in readable form so our moderation team can investigate them. The structured report is stored by Supabase; an included screen capture is sent to Sentry and linked by an event ID.

6. AI processing

To generate replies, the text of your messages and relevant context (such as connection profiles and retrieved "memory") are sent to DeepSeek, which generates every AI reply, summary, and suggestion in the app. Images you attach in chat are sent to DeepSeek so the reply can take them into account. The AI "memory" the app builds about you is stored on your device; short text excerpts may be sent to Google (Gemini) only to generate the numerical embeddings used for retrieval — Google does not receive your images and does not generate replies.

If you turn on the separate, off‑by‑default "Location‑aware suggestions" setting (Settings → CircleSage → Location context), a coarse location signal — your Home or Work label, the name of a saved place, or an approximate area — is included in the message sent to DeepSeek so replies can be more relevant. Your exact address is never included, and this is never sent unless the setting is on.

Place lookups

Opening a place's details, tapping a place chip, or the AI assistant mentioning a place in a chat reply can trigger an automatic lookup with HERE — including in the background, before you tap anything — using the place's name or address and, when available, an approximate bias derived from your current routine place (see Section 3), not your live location. If you then open that place's details, CircleSage also queries Tripadvisor using the resolved name (or, if needed, approximate coordinates) to show ratings, reviews, and photos alongside HERE's result. Neither provider receives an identifier tied to your account.

Product suggestions and affiliate links

When you use gift or product suggestions, we send a short product search phrase — not your chat history and not your identity — to the search providers and storefronts listed above.

Some product links in the app may carry an affiliate code. If you open one of these links and buy something, the storefront may pay us a commission. Three things follow from that, and we commit to all three:

  • It never changes the price you pay.
  • It does not influence which products the app suggests. Suggestions are ranked on how well they match what you asked for, never on how much commission they pay.
  • The affiliate code we attach is the same for every user. It carries no identifier of you, so a storefront cannot link a purchase back to your account, and we cannot either. We receive commission reports in aggregate only.

We record on our own servers that a product link was opened, together with the storefront it pointed to, so that we can check these reports. You can turn individual storefronts, or product suggestions entirely, off in the app's settings.

Apart from that, we do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell your personal data.

8. Location details and sharing

Raw location observations stay on your device (and in encrypted backups). A coarse label — such as "Home", "Work", or "Unknown location" — is synced to our backend. If you enable the relevant permission for a linked partner, that coarse label may be shared with them so they know your general context.

Sharing the name of a place is a separate, per‑connection permission that is off by default. When you turn it on for a linked partner, the name resolved on your device for a favourite place — such as a coffee shop, a restaurant, or another favourite spot — is synced and shown to that partner instead of the generic label. Home and Work are never shared as a name, no matter what this permission is set to. The stored name expires on the same schedule as the rest of your routine state, no later than 12 hours after it was observed, and it is deleted from our backend as soon as no linked connection has this permission enabled.

Proximity suggestions are separate. Only when both you and the linked partner explicitly enable them may the app temporarily sync a precise coordinate representing the center of each person's currently matched routine place. The backend uses these coordinates to compare distance; it does not show the coordinates to the linked partner. Each coordinate expires no later than 12 hours after it was observed and is removed sooner when the required sharing permission is no longer enabled or no current routine place is known. You can turn these permissions off at any time.

9. Data retention

We keep personal data only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. How long depends on where the data lives:

  • On your device: your profile, connections, conversations, AI "memory", and routine places are kept locally for as long as the app remains installed, or until you delete that item yourself in the app. Uninstalling the app removes this local copy. You can also clear the AI's learned "memory" at any time from within the app.
  • Encrypted backups and other account data on our backend: encrypted backups of your conversations, messages, and routine places; plain‑text connection data used for partner linking; your device/notification registration; and your subscription identifier are retained for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account (Profile → Account → Delete account in the app, or by emailing tuandiep98usa@gmail.com) permanently and immediately removes this data from our backend.
  • Period tracking data is retained on our backend for as long as the connection and its period‑tracking milestone exist. Removing the milestone, the connection, or your account permanently deletes it.
  • Routine‑place center coordinates and shared favourite‑place names (Section 8) are retained for no more than 12 hours after observation and are removed sooner when the required sharing permission is disabled or no current routine place is known.
  • AI response reports and moderation records: these exist so our moderation team can investigate potential policy violations. They are kept separately from the rest of your account and are retained for as long as necessary to review and act on the report and to meet our legal and safety obligations — including after you delete your account. A screen capture included with a report is held by Sentry under Sentry's own retention practices.
  • Crash reports: collected only in production builds and not linked to your identity, they are retained by our crash‑reporting provider (Sentry) only for as long as needed to diagnose and fix issues, after which they are deleted automatically.

When you delete your account we remove the backend data listed above; data is kept longer only where this section says so (moderation records) or where the law requires it.
