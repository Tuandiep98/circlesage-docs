CircleSage ("CircleSage", "we", "us") is a relationship‑companion app that offers

AI‑assisted coaching, reminders, and tools to help you nurture the relationships

around you. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes,

and the choices you have.

If you have any questions, contact us at tuandiep98usa@gmail.com.

1. Accounts work without a login

CircleSage does not ask you to create a username or password. When you first open

the app, we create an anonymous account with our backend provider (Supabase).

This account is tied to the app installation, not to your identity.

To help you recover your data after reinstalling or switching devices, the app may

send a one‑way SHA‑256 hash of basic device characteristics (for example, the

device model and vendor identifier) to our backend. We do not receive the raw

identifiers themselves. On iOS, the encryption key used for your encrypted backups

may be stored in the iCloud Keychain, so it can sync across your own Apple

devices.

2. Information you provide

  • Your profile: display name, birthday, an optional work/role label, and a

short description you choose to add.

  • People you add ("connections"): information you enter about the people in your

life — such as their name, relationship, birthday, interests, milestones, and

optional notes. This can include sensitive details like allergens, foods to

avoid, and personal quirks. You should only enter information about another person

if you have their permission to do so.

  • Chat messages: anything you type in conversations with the AI assistant.

3. Information collected automatically

  • Location (precise, including in the background): with your permission, the app

monitors your device location and physical‑activity signals to learn your

"routine places" (for example, where you usually spend time). This helps the app

offer relevant, time‑aware suggestions. Raw coordinates are processed and stored

on your device; place names are resolved on‑device. See Section 7 for what

leaves your device.

  • Device and notification data: a randomly generated device ID, push

notification token, platform, timezone, and language — used to deliver

notifications and sync your settings.

  • Subscription data: if you purchase a subscription, our payments partner

(RevenueCat) processes your purchase using an app‑specific account identifier.

4. How we use your data

We use your data to: provide AI coaching and suggestions; deliver reminders and

notifications; personalize responses based on your profile and connections; enforce

usage quotas; and maintain and improve the app.

5. Where your data is stored

  • On your device: most of your data — your profile, connections, conversations,

AI "memory", and routine places — lives in a local database on your device.

  • Encrypted cloud backup: your conversations, messages, and routine places are

encrypted on your device (AES‑256‑GCM) before being uploaded to our backend.

We store only the ciphertext and cannot read this content.

  • Connection data for partner linking: when you link with another CircleSage user,

the relevant connection details are stored on our backend in plain text so both

linked people can see the shared information. Only link with people you trust with

that information.

6. AI processing

To generate replies, the text of your messages and relevant context (such as

connection profiles and retrieved "memory") are sent to Google (Gemini) and, as

a fallback for certain features, DeepSeek. Images you attach in chat are sent to

Google for analysis via our backend. The AI "memory" the app builds about you is

stored on your device; short text excerpts may be sent to Google to generate the

numerical embeddings used for retrieval.

7. Third‑party services

We share data with the following providers only as needed to run the app:

Provider What it receives
Supabase (backend & database) Anonymous account, device registration, encrypted backups, plain‑text connection data for linking
Google Gemini Chat text, context, and text excerpts for embeddings
DeepSeek Prompt text, as a fallback for certain features
Google Places Place text you type when searching for a location
OpenWeatherMap Location text you type when requesting weather
Google Cloud Text‑to‑Speech Text you ask the app to read aloud
Firebase Cloud Messaging Push notification token, to deliver notifications
RevenueCat An app‑specific account ID, to manage subscriptions
Sentry Crash reports and basic device information, in production builds only

We use Sentry to receive crash reports so we can diagnose and fix errors. Crash

reports contain technical details about the failure and your device, are not linked to

your identity, and are collected only in production builds of the app.

Apart from that, we do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell

your personal data.

8. Location details and sharing

Your precise coordinates stay on your device (and in encrypted backups). Only a

coarse label — such as "Home", "Work", or "Unknown location" — is synced to our

backend. If you enable the relevant permission for a linked partner, that coarse

label may be shared with them so they know your general context. You can turn this

off at any time.

9. Data retention and deletion

Because your primary data lives on your device, uninstalling the app removes the

local copy. You can clear the AI's learned "memory" from within the app. To request

deletion of data associated with your account on our backend, contact us at

tuandiep98usa@gmail.com.

10. Children

CircleSage is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect

data from children under this age. If you believe a child has provided us data,

contact us and we will remove it.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your

personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these

rights, contact us at tuandiep98usa@gmail.com.

12. Security

We use client‑side encryption for backups of your conversations and routine data,

and rely on reputable providers for storage and transmission. No method of storage

or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

13. International transfers

Our providers (including Google and Supabase) may process and store data on servers

located outside your country, including in the United States. By using the app you

understand your data may be transferred internationally.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the

"Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
